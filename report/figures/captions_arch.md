## arch_final_model

**Caption.** Architecture of the final model: a DINOv2-B vision transformer (ViT-B/14) used as a frozen feature extractor with a trained linear classifier (linear probe). A 224 × 224 RGB image is split into 16 × 16 patches of 14 × 14 pixels, embedded to 768 dimensions, prefixed with a learnable [CLS] token and passed through 12 transformer blocks; the normalised [CLS] feature (768) feeds a linear layer with 2 outputs and a softmax. Only the linear layer (1,538 weights) is trained, with cross-entropy (label smoothing 0.1), AdamW and a cosine schedule for 10 epochs; the 85.7 M backbone parameters stay frozen. Shapes under each block are the stage outputs (batch dimension omitted).

**Alt text.** Flow diagram of seven boxes from input image to softmax output, with the four backbone boxes in grey (frozen) and the linear head in green (trained); tensor shapes 3×224×224, 256×768, 257×768, 257×768, 768, 2 and 2 are written under the boxes.

## arch_scratch_cnn

**Caption.** Architecture of the scratch CNN (model M1). Five convolutional blocks, each two 3 × 3 convolutions with batch normalisation and ReLU followed by 2 × 2 max-pooling, halve the 128 × 128 input to 4 × 4 while the channels grow 32 → 64 → 128 → 256 → 256; global average pooling gives a 256-dimensional feature, followed by dropout (0.3), a linear layer to 2 logits and a softmax. All 2.35 M parameters are trained from He initialisation with SGD (Nesterov momentum 0.9), a one-cycle schedule (peak LR 0.05), weight decay 5e-4, batch 128, 50 epochs and cross-entropy with label smoothing 0.1.

**Alt text.** Flow diagram of nine boxes from a 3×128×128 input through five convolutional blocks (32×64×64 down to 256×4×4), global average pooling (256), a dropout-plus-linear head and a softmax.
