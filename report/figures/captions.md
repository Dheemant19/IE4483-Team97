# Figure captions and alt text (draft)

Generated by scripts/make_plots.py. The figures carry no titles or explanations, so each caption must explain the encoding. Exact plotted values are in data/<figure>.csv.

## datasize_curve

**Caption.** Validation error of four Dogs vs. Cats models trained on class-balanced subsets of the training set (2,000 to 20,000 images; evaluated on the same 4,999 validation images). Large markers and lines: mean over 3 seeds; small open markers: individual seeds. Both axes are logarithmic. The value after each model name is its mean validation accuracy with 20,000 training images. FT: full fine-tuning; probe: frozen backbone with a trained linear head. Colour gives the training strategy as in the model comparison figure.

**Alt text.** Log-log line chart of validation error against training-set size. The scratch CNN's error falls from about 13% at 2,000 images to 2.4% at 20,000; ResNet-50 falls from 1.2% to 0.55%; ConvNeXt-T and the DINOv2-B probe stay roughly flat near 0.3% and 0.15%.

## model_ladder

**Caption.** Every Dogs vs. Cats model on one logarithmic error axis (4,999 validation images, last training epoch). One row per backbone, sorted by its best error; blue circles: full fine-tuning, green squares: frozen backbone with a trained linear head (linear probe), vermillion triangles: no pretraining. Large markers: mean over 3 seeds; small open markers: individual seeds; pale bar: seed range (HOG + SVM is deterministic, n = 1). Numbers are mean validation accuracy (%).

**Alt text.** Dot plot with one row per backbone on a log error axis, fine-tuned and linear-probe results on two sub-rows: HOG with a linear SVM at 68.29% accuracy, with an RBF SVM at 80.06%, the scratch CNN at 97.65%, and every pretrained backbone above 99.45%, the best being the DINOv2-B linear probe at 99.86%.

## aug_ablation

**Caption.** Training-time augmentation for the scratch CNN (128 px input, 50 epochs unless stated; 4,999 validation images). None: resize and centre crop only; crop + flip: random resized crop and horizontal flip; default: crop + flip plus rotation, colour jitter and random erasing. The dotted line marks the no-augmentation run. Large marker: mean; small open markers: seeds and pale bar: seed range (n = 3 for the default; n = 1 otherwise). Numbers are validation accuracy (%).

**Alt text.** Dot plot of scratch-CNN validation accuracy for five augmentation settings: 94.56% without augmentation, 97.72% with crop and flip, 97.65% for the default, 97.50% with TrivialAugment and 97.90% for the default trained 100 epochs.

## lr_sweep

**Caption.** Backbone learning-rate sweep for fine-tuning ResNet-50 on Dogs vs. Cats (AdamW, head LR 1e-3; epoch 0, shaded, trains only the new linear head, then 9 epochs fine-tune the whole network with warm-up and cosine decay; seed 0; 4,999 validation images). (a) Validation accuracy per epoch for each backbone LR. (b) Last-epoch accuracy against the backbone LR (log axis); the dashed line is the accuracy after the head-only epoch, which no fine-tuning run regains. Single seed: differences below about 0.1 point (5 images) are within seed-to-seed variation.

**Alt text.** Two panels sharing a validation-accuracy axis. Left: all four learning rates start at 99.58% after the head-only epoch, drop at epoch 1 when the backbone is unfrozen, and recover, the smallest LR least. Right: final accuracy rises with LR from 98.98% at 1e-5 to 99.52% at 3e-4, below the 99.58% head-only line.

## training_curves

**Caption.** Training curves on Dogs vs. Cats. (a) Scratch CNN, 50 epochs with a one-cycle LR schedule: training error is measured on the augmented training images, validation error on the 4,999 clean validation images; line: mean over 3 seeds, band: range across seeds; log axis. (b) Validation error of the pretrained models fine-tuned with linear probing then fine-tuning (seed 0): epoch 0 (shaded) trains only the new linear head; unfreezing the backbone at epoch 1 temporarily raises the error before it recovers.

**Alt text.** Two panels. Left: scratch CNN training and validation error falling from about 35% to 1.7% and 2.4% over 50 epochs. Right: validation error per epoch for five pretrained models, lowest after the head-only epoch 0, rising at epoch 1 and recovering to 0.2-0.6% by epoch 9.

## cifar_h

Ratio 10: Cross-entropy 88.9%, Focal loss 89.0%, Re-sampling 88.8%, Class-balanced + DRW 89.3%, Logit adjustment 89.9%.

## cifar_h_groups

**Caption.** Where the gains come from on long-tailed CIFAR-10 (imbalance ratio 100, ResNet-18 from scratch): test accuracy averaged over the 3 largest classes (head, 4,500 to 1,617 training images), the 4 middle classes (medium, 969 to 208) and the 3 smallest classes (tail, 125 to 45). Large markers: mean; small open markers and pale bars: seeds and their range (n = 3 for cross-entropy, class-balanced + DRW and logit adjustment; n = 1 for focal loss and re-sampling). Lines only connect the groups of one method. Numbers are tail accuracy (%).

**Alt text.** Slope chart of head, medium and tail class accuracy for five methods. All methods reach 94-95% on head classes; on tail classes logit adjustment reaches about 65%, class-balanced weighting 57%, cross-entropy 52%, focal loss 50% and re-sampling 41%.
